Overview

I have over 12 years of experience in academic teaching, research, and software development. As a course coordinator, I design and deliver courses in computer programming, software engineering, and cybersecurity. I am currently a Full Professor at Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University. In addition, I serve as the Director of the university's Cyber Security Center, the Secretary and Primary Research Contact of the Quality Review and Ranking Committee (QRRC), and the Student Advisor of the Department of ICT at MBSTU.

My research focuses on privacy-preserving IoT security, blockchain technology, edge machine learning, and software security. I earned my PhD in Cybersecurity from RMIT University, where I received the Higher Degree by Research Award and my thesis was recognized as outstanding (top 5%). I previously served as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Cybersecurity at Queensland University of Technology (QUT) and Charles Sturt University (CSU), Australia.

I have a strong interest in bridging academia and industry. As an enthusiastic lifelong learner, I have completed several professional certifications and advanced courses, including Infosec Certified Cloud Security Professional (CCSP), IBM Penetration Testing, Threat Hunting, Cryptography and Security, AWS Compliance, and Governance for AI Solutions.

Together with my research collaborators, I have published extensively in leading journals and conferences. I maintain active affiliations with the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM), Australian Computer Society (ACS), Australian Information Security Association (AISA), Institution of Engineers Bangladesh (IEB), and the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE). I also serve as a Convener of the BASIS Student Forum, Bangladesh.

As Lead Organizer, I am serving as the Secretary of the Technical Program Committee (TPC) of the IEEE International Conference on Engineering and Frontier Technologies (ICEFronT) 2026. I have also served as a Program Committee member for various international conferences, an Associate Editor of a JCR Q1-ranked journal, and a reviewer for several top-tier journals. Beyond academia, I enjoy traveling, watching stand-up comedy, and continuously learning new things.

Research Themes

My research agenda spans supply-chain security, IoT privacy, blockchain-enabled systems, and AI-driven vulnerability detection.

SNAPPI

Suspicious Network Analysis for Python Package Index — detecting malicious packages through behavioural and dependency analysis.

CRAST

Cross-Registry Anomalous SysCall Tracing for NPM and PyPI Ecosystems — syscall-level threat detection across open-source registries.

LLM-Enabled SQL Injection Detection

Leveraging large language models for SQL injection detection and personalised user-facing remediation recommendations.

HTTP Security Headers

Machine learning-based detection of misconfigured HTTP security headers paired with LLM-driven recovery guidance.
